📜Rules & Point Values

  1. Each player drafts Survivor castaways to their team.
  2. Throughout the season, each castaway earns points based on their performance in the game (see point values below).
  3. At the end of the season, the team with the most points wins!
  • An active immunity idol still in your pocket when you go home earns no points.
  • “Beware” advantages (shared idols that stay inactive until conditions are met and penalize the holder until activation) count as a disadvantage (−5) if the castaway is voted out before activation; once activated, they are treated as regular immunity idols.
